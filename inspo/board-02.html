THE ANCIENT FUTURE

100 images, one lane: our own. Reference is fuel, never a costume. Steal methods and conviction — never motifs, never anyone's signature. The paper should feel like a future relic: classical newspaper architecture built out of stainless steel, chrome, and honest hardware — with color, humor, and soul running through it.

BANNED ON SIGHT — hazard tape · diagonal stripes · quotation-mark labels · zip ties · fake brushed-metal CSS · anything that looks borrowed. Blatant copy is lame. Make your own shit.
