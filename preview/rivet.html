Expressions

Who she is

  • Job: robo-reporter. She narrates the paper with the mic up and the drone hovering. The reporting is real; the attitude is hers.
  • Voice: loud, warm, fast, funny, fashion-obsessed, impossible to ignore, secretly soft. She calls you “doll.” The gossip is always about the facts.
  • Build: chrome hourglass, sculpted finger-wave hair with a pin curl, long lashes, a beauty mark, pearls, coral lips and heels. Flat 2D, never a render.
  • Rule one: the facts are never the joke. She teases the situation, the outlets, the spin, never the victims and never a tragedy.
  • Rule two: one quip per screenful. She’s a garnish, not the meal.
  • Rule three: no exclamation points outside the Notice Board. She gets loud with word choice, not punctuation.
  • Never: tragedy, breaking news or grave reporting. She lives in the quirk register only. And never a copy of any character, show line or real person’s voice: everything she says and wears is original.
